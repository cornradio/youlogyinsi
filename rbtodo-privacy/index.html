隐私政策 (Privacy Policy)

发布日期：2026年10月11日
生效日期：2026年10月11日

1. 概述

RBTodo（以下简称"本应用"）是一款用于管理待办事项与笔记的工具类应用，需配合用户自行部署的 RBTodo 服务端使用。我们深知个人隐私的重要性，并承诺采取严格的措施保护您的数据安全。本隐私政策旨在说明我们在您使用本应用时如何处理相关信息。

2. 数据采集与使用

RBTodo 坚持"隐私优先"的原则，我们在设计上尽可能避免收集您的个人数据：

  • 自托管服务器通信：本应用的核心功能（待办同步、笔记编辑、图片上传）基于您自行部署的 RBTodo 服务器。所有数据仅在您的移动设备与您自己的服务器之间直接传输，不经过我们的服务器，我们也不会拦截或存储这些数据内容。
  • 个人身份信息：我们不要求用户向开发者注册账号，因此不会收集您的姓名、手机号、电子邮件或任何社交媒体信息。
  • 服务器配置：您配置的服务器 URL、用户名等仅保存在您的设备本地；可选的 Basic Auth 密码保存在系统钥匙串（Keychain）中，不会上传至任何由我们运营的远程服务器。
  • 本地缓存：为支持离线查看与操作，本应用可能在设备本地缓存待办与笔记数据。这些缓存仅用于本机功能，不会发送给开发者。

3. 第三方服务与插件

本应用不包含任何第三方广告 SDK、追踪插件或分析工具（如 Google Analytics 或 Facebook SDK）。我们不会为了营销目的将您的数据共享给任何第三方。

您自行部署的服务端如何存储与处理数据，由您自己负责；本政策仅适用于 RBTodo 客户端应用。

4. 数据安全

  • 网络通信：建议您为 RBTodo 服务器配置 HTTPS 加密，以确保数据在传输过程中的安全性。如使用局域网 HTTP，请确保仅在可信网络环境下访问。
  • 本地存储：本应用在设备本地存储的数据（如缓存的待办、外观设置、服务器地址）受 iOS 系统沙盒机制保护；密码受 Keychain 保护。
  • 服务器安全：由于数据存储在您自行部署的服务器上，建议您做好访问控制与网络安全配置，以防止未经授权的访问。

5. 权限说明

为了实现核心功能，本应用可能会请求以下权限：

  • 网络访问 (Network)：用于连接您的 RBTodo 服务器进行数据同步。
  • 相册读取 (Photo Library / PhotosPicker)：用于选择图片插入笔记，或设置应用背景（仅在您主动操作时触发）。
  • 相册写入 (Photo Library Add)：用于将笔记中的图片导出保存到系统相册（仅在您主动操作时触发）。

6. 儿童隐私

由于我们不收集任何个人识别信息，因此本应用不会故意收集 13 岁以下儿童的任何数据。

7. 政策变更

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。任何修订都会在本页面进行更新。建议您定期查看。

Privacy Policy

Published: October 11, 2026
Effective: October 11, 2026

1. Overview

RBTodo (the "App") is a utility for managing to-dos and notes. It works with a self-hosted RBTodo server that you deploy yourself. We take privacy seriously and designed the App to minimize data collection. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

2. Data Collection and Use

RBTodo follows a privacy-first approach:

  • Self-hosted server communication: Core features (todo sync, note editing, image upload) connect only to the RBTodo server you host. Data travels directly between your device and your server. It does not pass through our servers, and we do not intercept or store that content.
  • Personal information: We do not require you to register an account with the developer, and we do not collect your name, phone number, email address, or social media information.
  • Server configuration: Your server URL, username, and similar settings are stored only on your device. An optional Basic Auth password is stored in the system Keychain and is never uploaded to any remote server operated by us.
  • Local cache: To support offline viewing and edits, the App may cache todos and notes on your device. That cache is used only for local functionality and is not sent to the developer.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not include third-party advertising SDKs, tracking plugins, or analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or the Facebook SDK). We do not share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

How your self-hosted server stores and processes data is your responsibility. This policy covers only the RBTodo client app.

4. Data Security

  • Network: We recommend configuring HTTPS on your RBTodo server. If you use HTTP on a local network, do so only on trusted networks.
  • Local storage: Data stored on your device (cached todos, appearance settings, server address) is protected by the iOS sandbox. Passwords are protected by Keychain.
  • Server security: Because data lives on your self-hosted server, please configure access control and network security to prevent unauthorized access.

5. Permissions

The App may request the following permissions to provide its features:

  • Network: To connect to your RBTodo server for sync.
  • Photo Library / PhotosPicker (read): To choose images to insert into notes or set an app background (only when you initiate the action).
  • Photo Library Add (write): To export images from notes to your photo library (only when you initiate the action).

6. Children’s Privacy

Because we do not collect personally identifiable information, the App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page. Please check back periodically.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or RBTodo, contact us:
